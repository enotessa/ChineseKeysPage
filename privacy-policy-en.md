# Privacy Policy for the Chinese Keys App

Effective date: August 18, 2026.

The app stores the user's name, preferred form of address, settings, and learning progress locally on the device. The developer does not receive this information and does not create user accounts.

Microphone permission is used only after an explicit user action in a pronunciation exercise. Audio is passed to the system speech recognition service selected on the device. The app does not store audio files or send them to the developer.

The app uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. The advertising SDK may process device or advertising identifiers, advertising interaction data, diagnostics, and other technical information in accordance with the Yandex Privacy Policy and the consent selected by the user.

The app uses MyTracker SDK to analyze app usage and measure user acquisition effectiveness. It may process device and app identifiers, device and network information, launches, sessions, and in-app interactions. Custom analytics events do not contain the user's name, preferred form of address, search queries, handwriting paths, audio recordings, or speech recognition results. Location tracking is not enabled in MyTracker settings.

The app does not request precise or approximate location.

Users can delete locally stored data by clearing the app's data in the device settings or by uninstalling the app.
