# Google Play listing — English

## Title

Chinese Keys

## Short description

Learn Chinese radicals through imagery, writing, pronunciation, and review.

## Full description

Chinese Keys helps you understand the building blocks of Chinese characters.

Use the app to:

- learn all 214 Kangxi radicals with pinyin and translations;
- remember shapes through clear visual associations;
- listen to Chinese pronunciation and recognize tones;
- practise handwriting and stroke order;
- find familiar radicals inside characters;
- retain knowledge through spaced repetition;
- track learning streaks and progress.

Learning progress is stored locally on your device. Microphone permission is requested only when you choose to use a pronunciation exercise.
